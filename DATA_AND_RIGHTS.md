# Data and Rights

## Included

- author-written paper-level analysis and figure-generation code;
- derived monthly portfolio-return and robustness evidence used by the working paper;
- author-generated reference figures and tables;
- source-version hashes and clean-replay identity information; and
- numerical and output-hash validation checks.

## Not redistributed

- downloaded raw ETF/market-price source files;
- downloaded FRED or FRED-MD source files;
- credentials, local machine paths, caches, or session artifacts;
- private research notes or project-control files; and
- third-party papers or other copyrighted source documents.

The derived evidence is supplied to reproduce the reported paper-level results. Users should consult the respective upstream providers for terms governing redistribution of underlying market and macroeconomic source data.
